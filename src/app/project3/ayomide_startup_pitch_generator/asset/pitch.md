## 2. The Core Problem: The Early-Stage GTM Bottleneck

Great code dies in obscurity because technical founders face a fatal distribution triad:

* **The Agency Trap:** Hiring traditional growth agencies costs $7,000–$15,000/month with 3-month ramp-up times, mediocre deliverables, and zero technical empathy for complex developer tools.
* **Point-Tool Fatigue:** Modern marketing requires managing 8 to 14 disconnected SaaS applications (SEO audits, copy generators, social schedulers, newsletter platforms, analytics suites). Stitching these together turns founders into manual workflow operators instead of product visionaries.
* **The Context Void:** Point-solution AI tools only generate ungrounded, generic text. They do not understand the live code, shifting product features, competitor moves, or closed-loop conversion metrics of a scaling SaaS.

Result? 90% of early-stage SaaS ventures fail not because of product defects, but because of go-to-market starvation.
